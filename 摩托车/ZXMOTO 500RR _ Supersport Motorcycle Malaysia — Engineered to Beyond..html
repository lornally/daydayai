500RR Overview

Engineered to Beyond.

ZXMOTO's debut model and the one that started it all. The 500RR was unveiled at the 2024 China International Motorcycle Exhibition and went on to sell over 25,000 units in its first year. A 470cc inline-four screaming to 13,500rpm, 0–100 in 3.4 seconds, and a 235 km/h top speed that embarrasses bikes twice its price.
